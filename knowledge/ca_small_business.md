# California small business: starting map

Where to look first. Confirm everything against current official sources before
relying on it.

## LLCs
- **Corporations Code 17701.01 et seq.**: California Revised Uniform LLC Act (formation, operating agreements, member duties).
- **Secretary of State (bizfileOnline.sos.ca.gov)**: Articles of Organization (LLC-1); Statement of Information (LLC-12) due shortly after formation and then every two years.
- **Franchise Tax Board**: annual LLC tax (Rev. & Tax. Code 17941) plus a gross-receipts fee above set thresholds (17942). Check FTB for current amounts and any first-year rules.
- **Operating agreement**: not filed with the state, but every LLC should have one, especially with more than one member.

## Selling goods (apparel, merch)
- **CDTFA seller's permit**: required to sell tangible goods in California, including online and at pop-ups. Collect and remit sales tax.
- **Resale certificate**: lets the business buy inventory for resale without paying sales tax.
- **Fictitious Business Name (DBA)**: filed with the county if trading under a name other than the LLC's exact name.
- **Local business license**: city or county.

## Intellectual property (creative / media / apparel)
- Trademarks: USPTO federal registration; California state registration through the Secretary of State.
- Copyright: designs, photos and music; U.S. Copyright Office.
- Contracts to watch: designer and contractor agreements (who owns the work), manufacturer agreements, collaboration and licensing deals, influencer agreements.

## Employment (once hiring)
- Contractor versus employee: California's ABC test (Labor Code 2775 et seq.).
- Payroll registration with EDD, workers' comp insurance, wage statements (Labor Code 226).

## Certifications that open contracts and funding
- CPUC Supplier Clearinghouse (women- and minority-owned; free).
- SBA Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB).
- WBENC (women-owned) and NMSDC (minority-owned).
