# Midnight 06 LLC: funding plan

Researched October 22, 2026, for an Orange County, California creative/media/apparel LLC that is
woman-owned and minority-owned. Deadlines change often and third-party grant
lists are often wrong, so **confirm every deadline on the funder's own site
before applying.** To refresh this list, run:

```
python -m paralegal ask "Find grants, loans and certifications for Midnight 06 LLC, a woman- and minority-owned creative/media/apparel LLC in California. Focus on what is open in the next 90 days."
```

## Do these first (free, and they unlock more money)

| Step | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the LLC in good standing: Statement of Information filed, $800 FTB tax paid, seller's permit if selling apparel | Most grants check this. A suspended LLC can't win anything | bizfileOnline.sos.ca.gov, ftb.ca.gov, cdtfa.ca.gov |
| Get an EIN and a separate business bank account | Required by nearly every grant and loan | irs.gov |
| **CPUC Supplier Clearinghouse certification**: free for 51%+ women-, minority- or LGBT-owned businesses, good for 3 years | Lets California utilities count purchases from you toward their 15% minority and 5% women supplier goals. Good for merch and creative/media contracts | thesupplierclearinghouse.com |
| SBA Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) certification | Access to set-aside federal contracts | certify.sba.gov |
| WBENC (women) and NMSDC (minority) certification | Corporate supplier-diversity programs, and some grants require one | wbenc.org, nmsdc.org |
| Make free profiles on **Hello Alice** and the **IFundWomen universal grant application** | They match you to new rounds automatically. Hello Alice doesn't publish rounds in advance | helloalice.com, ifundwomen.com |
| Visit your local **Women's Business Center** or **SBDC** | Free advisors, help with applications, and early notice of local grants | sba.gov/local-assistance |

## Orange County
- **OCIE SBDC** (hosted by Cal State Fullerton): free one-on-one consulting and
  workshops, in 20+ languages. Book an advisor first. They hear about local
  grants before they're widely posted.
- **OC Women's Business Center**: free accelerator workshops run with the SBDC
  (sessions have been held at OC Workforce Solutions in Brea).
- **County and city grants**: there's no open Orange County or city grant for
  2026 right now. The county's $2,500 microbusiness grant ended in 2022. Check
  the economic development pages for your city (Santa Ana, Anaheim, Irvine) a
  few times a year.
- **California Arts Council fellowships** are run by region. Apply through the
  organization that covers Orange County when the 2027 window opens.

## Not available
- **California Dream Fund**: ended. Its training closed Dec 31, 2022. Ignore sites still advertising it.
- The state small business office has no grant program that pays businesses directly right now. State money currently comes through tax credits, loans and regional innovation hubs.

## Avoiding grant scams
Real grants don't ask for bank logins, don't make you pay a "processing" or
"release" fee to receive money, and don't contact you out of the blue saying
you've won. Small application fees ($15-$25) exist at a few legitimate programs
such as the Amber Grant. Check every one before paying.
