# Application tracker

Who does what:
- **Claude:** finds programs, checks eligibility, drafts every answer, prepares
  the packet and reminds you of deadlines.
- **You:** create the accounts, upload ID and financial documents, sign the
  statements, pay any fees and click submit. Applications ask you to swear the
  information is true, so you have to be the one submitting.

Status: ⬜ not started · ✍️ drafting · 📤 ready for you to submit · ✅ submitted · 🏆 won · ❌ closed or not a fit
