# Forming Midnight 06 LLC this week

Do these in order. Several grants (Breva, Hello Alice, IFundWomen) need a formed
business, and you'll need the LLC to sign contracts with festivals and hotels.

## Day 1: check the name (15 min)
- [ ] Search "Midnight 06" in the Secretary of State business search: bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov
- [ ] Search the USPTO trademark database (tmsearch.uspto.gov) for "Midnight 06" and close variations in entertainment and photography services. A conflict now is much cheaper to fix than later
- [ ] Check that the handles are free on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, and grab them all the same day

## Day 1: file Articles of Organization, Form LLC-1 (30 min, $70)
- [ ] File online at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov
- [ ] Management: "member-managed" is usual for two founders who both run the business
- [ ] Registered agent: one of you, at a California street address (it becomes public), or a paid registered agent service to keep your home address private
- [ ] Save the filed PDF. Grants ask for it

## After approval: EIN (10 min, free)
- [ ] irs.gov/ein. Apply directly with the IRS; never pay a website for this
- [ ] Save the confirmation letter (CP 575)

## Operating agreement (most important for two founders)
Not filed with the state, but **don't skip it.** It decides:
- Ownership split and who controls what
- **Who owns the photos and videos**. Make it the LLC, so the content library stays with the business if someone leaves
- How money is split, and what happens if one founder wants out
- How you make big decisions (travel spending, signing contracts)

The paralegal team in this repo can draft it. Ask:
```
python -m paralegal ask "Draft a California two-member LLC operating agreement for Midnight 06 LLC, a member-managed creative duo (photographer/videographer and dancer). Include IP ownership of content by the LLC, equal voting, buyout terms if a member leaves, and spending approval rules."
```
Have a California business attorney review it before you both sign. The OC SBDC can point you to low-cost help.

## Within 90 days
- [ ] Statement of Information (LLC-12), $20, at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov
- [ ] Business bank account (bring the LLC-1, EIN and operating agreement)
- [ ] Irvine business license (City of Irvine website)

## Money to plan for
- LLC-1 filing: $70
- Statement of Information: $20
- **California franchise tax: $800 a year**, first payment due by the 15th day of the 4th month after formation. Confirm the current rules at ftb.ca.gov. Budget for this; missing it can get the LLC suspended, and a suspended LLC can't win grants

## Legal heads-ups for your business (to check before you book shows)
- **Contracts:** get every gig in writing: fee, travel, accommodation, and who owns and can use the content
- **Festival media rules:** most festivals restrict professional cameras without media credentials. Apply for press or media passes, or get permission from the promoter or artist who hires you
- **Model and location releases:** filming people and private property for paid content usually needs releases
- **International gigs (Egypt and others):** performing or working for pay abroad can require a work permit or visa. Check with the promoter and the country's consulate before you go
- **Insurance:** general liability insurance (and equipment insurance) is often required by venues
