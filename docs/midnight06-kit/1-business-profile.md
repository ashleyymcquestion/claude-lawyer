## What the business does
- **In one sentence:** Midnight 06 is a two-woman creative duo, a photographer/videographer and a shuffle dancer, that creates performance and content for the EDM world.
- **What we sell:** a duo package of live shuffle dance performance plus professional photo and video content, for festivals, events, artists, venues and hospitality brands
- **Who buys:** EDM festivals and promoters (small local events up to EDC, Ultra and international events like Starlight in Egypt), DJs and artists, event venues, and luxury, boutique and short-term-rental hospitality near festival venues that needs fresh content
- **What's different:** we perform *and* film, so one booking delivers both the show and the content. We also want to pair festivals with nearby luxury and boutique stays to turn attending a festival into a full experience, and to build a community of creators in the scene
- **Why "Midnight 06":** all the best times start after midnight
- **Long-term vision:** travel the world's EDM festivals creating content, build a team and community of creators in this niche, then pay local artists and give back

## Note on "minority-owned"
Most minority-owned certifications (NMSDC, CPUC Supplier Clearinghouse) need the
business to be **at least 51% owned and controlled by minority-group members**.
They also have their own rules for proving heritage, and some want a minimum
share of ancestry, so check with the certifier before applying.

With a 50/50 split, Midnight 06 counts as **women-owned** but probably not
**minority-owned**. That's fine, because every grant in this kit is open to
women-owned businesses. The ownership split is a real decision about control
and money, so make it for business reasons, not for a certification.

On applications, describe each founder's heritage only the way she personally
identifies.
