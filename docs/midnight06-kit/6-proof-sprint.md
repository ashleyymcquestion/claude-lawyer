# 9-day proof sprint (Oct 22-31)

WomensNet says winning applications tend to show four things:
1. **Evidence of market demand** (e.g. a product selling out)
2. **A founder with a track record**
3. **A personal connection** to the business
4. **A clear plan** for the money

Right now Midnight 06 has #3 and #4. This sprint gets you #1 and #2 before
Oct 31, so your application is about something that's already happening, not
just an idea. **None of it costs money.**

## Day 1-2: launch the socials with proof of skill
- [ ] Same handle everywhere (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)
- [ ] Post 3 videos made by the two of you: shuffle dance filmed and edited by your photographer, using your current camera or a phone. Good light and good edits beat expensive gear
- [ ] Bio: "Dancer + filmmaker duo · EDM performance & content · Irvine, CA · Booking: [email]"
- [ ] Pin your best video. Note the views and followers after 48 hours, because those numbers go in the application

## Day 2-6: get one "yes" from the industry
Send the message below to **at least 20** people:
- Local EDM promoters and event pages in Orange County and LA (find them through Instagram event posts and on Eventbrite or Posh)
- Up-and-coming DJs who play SoCal shows
- Boutique hotels and Airbnb hosts in Irvine, Costa Mesa, Long Beach and Las Vegas near venues
- Shuffle dance communities and crews

You're looking for any one of these:
- "Yes, come shoot our event" (even unpaid, with guest-list or media access)
- "Send us the content, we'll repost / use it"
- A short message saying they'd hire you later. Screenshot it

**Message template (DM or email):**
> Hi [NAME]! We're Midnight 06, a dancer + videographer duo from Irvine making
> content for the EDM scene. [ONE LINK TO YOUR BEST VIDEO]
>
> We'd love to perform and shoot at [EVENT / your next show] and give you the
> edited photos and a recap reel to post, free, for this first one. We're
> building our portfolio and would be proud to have [EVENT/DJ] in it.
>
> Would you be open to it? We can send a quick plan.
> [NAME 1] & [NAME 2] · Midnight 06

**Version for hotels and Airbnb hosts:**
> Hi! We're Midnight 06, a dancer + videographer duo from Irvine who create
> content around EDM festivals. A lot of festival-goers book stays like yours.
> We'd love to shoot a short "festival weekend at [PROPERTY]" reel and photos
> you can use on your listing and socials, in exchange for a night's stay
> around [EVENT]. Here's our work: [LINK]. Interested?

## Day 3-7: write down your track record
Each founder lists anything real:
- Dance: years shuffling, classes, competitions, events danced at, follower counts on personal accounts, viral videos
- Photo/video: years shooting, paid or unpaid shoots, clients, events covered, editing software you know
- Anything showing you finish things: jobs, school, side businesses

## Day 7-8: update the application
- [ ] Add your numbers: views, followers, number of promoters contacted, every "yes"
- [ ] Add your track record: one sentence per founder
- [ ] Add the specific night story (see `applications/amber-grant.md`)

## Day 9 (Oct 30, not the 31st): submit
Submit a day early in case the site or payment has problems.
