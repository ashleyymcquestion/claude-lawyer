# Reusable grant answers (drafts)

Most applications ask some version of these questions. These are starting
drafts. Replace every [BRACKET] with your real details, and make sure the final
version sounds like you, because judges reward real stories and specifics.
Once the business profile is filled in, Claude can finish these and tailor them
to each grant.

## 1. Tell us about your business (about 150 words)
Midnight 06 is a woman- and minority-owned creative, media and apparel company
based in [CITY], Orange County, California. We make [PRODUCTS] for [CUSTOMERS].
The name comes from [STORY]. Since starting in [YEAR], we've [TRACTION: units
sold, events, followers, collaborations]. What sets us apart is [DIFFERENCE].

## 2. Why do you need this grant / how will you use the funds?
Be exact. Judges fund plans, not hopes.
> With $[AMOUNT], Midnight 06 will [SPECIFIC USE: e.g. produce our first
> 300-unit run of the (NAME) collection ($X), buy a heat press ($Y), and run a
> targeted ad campaign ($Z)]. We expect this to [RESULT: e.g. raise monthly
> revenue from $A to $B within six months].

## 3. What challenges have you faced as a [woman / minority] founder?
Answer honestly, in your own words, with one concrete example. Don't copy this
one. Questions to think about: access to capital, manufacturing minimums,
being taken seriously by vendors, and balancing work and family.

## 4. What impact will your business have on your community?
> [Hiring locally / showcasing local artists / giving back X% / events in
> Orange County / mentoring young creatives.] Give one number if you can.

## 5. Where do you see the business in 3-5 years?
> [Revenue goal, retail/wholesale partners, team size, product lines.]

> Midnight 06 is [WHAT] for [WHO] that [WHY IT MATTERS]. Founded by [YOUR NAME],
> a [YOUR BACKGROUND] in Orange County, we've [TRACTION] and are ready to [NEXT STEP].
