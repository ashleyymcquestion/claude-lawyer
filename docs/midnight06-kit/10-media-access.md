# Getting media access to festivals and raves

## How it actually works
- **Big festivals rarely credential independent creators.** EDC's FAQ says
  Insomniac doesn't issue media or photo credentials to independent
  photographers or videographers, and professional cameras aren't allowed
  for regular ticket holders. Other big festivals work similarly.
- **Who gets passes:** people on assignment for a publication, or hired by an
  artist, the festival, a sponsor or a brand. Press teams decide based on
  *who you're shooting for* and where the content will run.
- **Timing:** photo lists often close **4-8 weeks** before the event, and
  earlier for big stages. For EDC Las Vegas (May 2027), start in **January**.
- **Passes come with rules:** limited shooting windows, no flash, release
  forms. Read before signing; the paralegal team can review them.

## Seven ways in, easiest first

### 1. Small events and local promoters (start now)
Orange County, LA and Long Beach clubs, warehouse events, and smaller
promoters. Ask directly: "Can we shoot and perform in exchange for media
access? You get a recap reel." This is how you build the portfolio every
bigger route requires.

### 2. Festival Stays sidesteps the gate
Content at the partner hotel or Airbnb (getting ready, the pre-party,
heading out, after-hours) needs **no festival credential at all**. Inside the
festival, film with a phone or a small fixed-lens camera within the
festival's attendee camera rules.

### 3. Shoot for an EDM publication
Contribute to EDM blogs and outlets (national sites and SoCal or local
scene pages) as a photographer or video contributor. Once you're their
contributor, **they request credentials for you**. Start by pitching coverage
of smaller shows; once they've published your work, ask them to put you on
the list for bigger festivals.

### 4. Get hired by a DJ or artist
Up-and-coming DJs need content and often can't afford a crew. Shoot for them
at small shows, and once they play festivals their team can add you as their
photographer or videographer (artist credentials). **Creator Spotlight is
your pipeline:** feature small DJs now, and grow with them.

### 5. Brands and sponsors at festivals
Brands that set up at festivals (drinks, fashion, gear, hotels) hire content
creators. Pitch them with your media kit.

### 6. Performer casting
Festivals and event companies hire dancers and performance artists. Watch
festival and promoter social accounts and performer agencies for casting
calls, and keep a dance reel ready. This puts the dancer in as **talent**,
with access.

### 7. The festival's own content team
Big festivals hire photographers and videographers for their own content
(often through agencies or job postings). This is a later goal, once you
have a portfolio from routes 1-5.

## Templates

### A. To a local promoter
> Subject: Content + performance for [EVENT] on [DATE]
>
> Hi [NAME], we're Midnight 06, a dancer + videographer duo from Irvine
> creating content for the SoCal EDM scene: [LINK TO BEST VIDEO].
>
> We'd love to perform and shoot at [EVENT] in exchange for media access.
> You'd get a recap reel and [N] edited photos within [N] days to promote
> your next event. We'll follow your venue's rules and get releases from
> anyone featured.
>
> Media kit: [LINK]. Can we be on the list?
> [NAME 1] & [NAME 2]

### B. To an EDM publication (become a contributor)
> Subject: Photo/video contributor: SoCal EDM coverage
>
> Hi [EDITOR], we're Midnight 06, a photographer/videographer + dancer duo
> covering the Southern California EDM scene. Portfolio: [LINK].
>
> We'd like to contribute event coverage (photo galleries and short recap
> videos) for [OUTLET], starting with [SPECIFIC UPCOMING SHOW]. We can
> deliver within 48 hours of the show.
>
> Would you be open to us covering it for you? Happy to start with a trial
> piece.
> [NAME 1] & [NAME 2] · [EMAIL]

### C. Festival media credential request (only with an outlet or client)
> Subject: Media credential request: [OUTLET / CLIENT], [FESTIVAL] [DATES]
>
> Hello [FESTIVAL] press team,
>
> We're requesting [photo / video] credentials for [FESTIVAL] on [DATES] on
> behalf of [OUTLET or CLIENT, e.g. "DJ NAME's team" / "EDM OUTLET"].
>
> - Coverage: [what you'll produce: gallery, recap reel, artist content]
> - Where it runs: [outlet/channel] (audience: [numbers])
> - Assignment contact: [editor or manager name + email]
> - Portfolio: [LINK]
> - Crew: [NAME 1] (photo/video), [NAME 2] (assistant / talent)
>
> We'll follow all photo-pit and venue rules. Thank you for considering us.
> [NAME] · Midnight 06 LLC · [EMAIL] · [PHONE]

### D. To a DJ / artist manager
> Subject: Content for [DJ NAME]'s upcoming sets
>
> Hi [NAME], we're Midnight 06, a videographer + dancer duo. We'd love to
> shoot [DJ]'s set at [EVENT]: crowd energy, performance clips and a recap
> reel sized for TikTok and Instagram. [LINK]
>
> First shoot is on us. If you like it, we'd love to keep working together,
> including at festival dates where your team can credential us.
> [NAME 1] & [NAME 2]
