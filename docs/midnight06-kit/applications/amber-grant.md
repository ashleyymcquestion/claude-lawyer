# Amber Grant (WomensNet)

- **Official site:** ambergrantsforwomen.com, then "Apply Now"
- **Amount:** WomensNet's own pages now describe $15,000 monthly awards and a $75,000 year-end grant (older guides say $10K/$50K)
- **Deadline:** 11:59 PM Eastern on the last day of every month. **This round: Oct 31.**
- **Fee:** $15, non-refundable. Can't afford it? Email info@ambergrantsforwomen.com asking for a fee waiver **before** you apply
- **One application counts for every Amber grant**, including the category grants (there's a Fashion & Interiors category)

## Eligibility check
- [ ] Woman, 18+
- [ ] Owns at least 50% of the business
- [ ] Business is based in the US

## What the form asks, and your answers

The form asks about your business, what motivates you, the story behind it,
opportunities and challenges, and what you'd do with the money. They want
personality and heart, and they say a business idea is fine at any stage.

**Business name:** Midnight 06 LLC

**Tell us about your business / business idea:**

> based in [CITY], Orange County, California. We design [PRODUCTS, e.g. limited
> streetwear drops, graphic tees and visual content] for [CUSTOMERS]. [ONE
> TRACTION FACT, e.g. "Our first drop of 100 pieces sold out in 9 days."]

**What motivates you / the story behind your business:**
> [YOUR REAL STORY: why "Midnight 06"? What were you doing at midnight? Who are

**Opportunities and challenges:**
> The opportunity: [e.g. demand from our community is growing faster than we
> can produce; we've had X pre-orders or requests for wholesale]. The challenge:
> [e.g. manufacturers require minimum orders of X units, which costs $Y up
> front, so we've been producing in small batches funded out of pocket.]

**What would you do with the money?**
> With $10,000, we would:
> - $[X]: produce our next collection of [N] pieces at a lower per-unit cost
> - $[X]: [equipment, e.g. a heat press or a camera for content]
> - $[X]: [website, photography, or marketing for launch]
> - $[X]: [samples, trademark filing, or a pop-up booth]
> This would let us [RESULT, e.g. double our monthly sales and hire our first
> part-time helper by mid-2027].
