# Amber Grant (WomensNet): ready to submit

- **Apply:** ambergrantsforwomen.com, then "Apply Now"
- **Deadline:** Oct 31, 11:59 PM Eastern (8:59 PM in Irvine). **Submit Oct 30.**
- **Fee:** $15. For a fee waiver, email info@ambergrantsforwomen.com **before** applying
- **Who applies:** one founder, who must own **at least 50%**
- **How it's judged:** the WomensNet team reviews applications daily, picks monthly finalists, then discusses them and votes. Winners are announced by the 21st of the next month
- **One application covers every WomensNet grant** (monthly, startup and category grants), and it reportedly stays in the pool for category grants for up to 11 months

## Realistic odds
WomensNet doesn't publish applicant numbers. Outside estimates range from
400-700 to "thousands" of applications a month for three monthly awards, which
is roughly **1 in 150 to 1 in 1,000 per month**, before you count the
startup and category grants. Applications with proof of demand do better than
ideas alone, which is why `6-proof-sprint.md` exists.

**Re-applying:** WomensNet's own advice is to re-apply once the business has
*meaningfully improved*, not every month with the same text. Re-apply when you
have a real update: the LLC filed, a first booking, a jump in followers.
