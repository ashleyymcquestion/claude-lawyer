# Freed Fellowship

- **Official site:** freedfellowship.com, then "Get Started Now" (asks for your name and email first)
- **Amount:** $500 per month to one business, plus automatic consideration for a $2,500 year-end grant. No restrictions on how you use it, no reporting, no equity
- **Deadline:** rolling. Apply by 11:59 PM Eastern on the last day of the month to count for that month. **This round: Oct 31**
- **Fee:** a listing mentions $19. Confirm on the site
- **Bonus:** every applicant gets a "Freed score" with feedback, plus 2 months of Freed Studio (an online small-business community). **Use that feedback to make your other applications stronger.**

## Eligibility check
- [ ] US micro or small business that already exists
- [ ] Underrepresented founders (women, minorities) are especially encouraged
