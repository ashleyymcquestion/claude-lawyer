# Always-on profiles (set up once, get matched to new grants)

## Hello Alice (helloalice.com)
Free. Rounds often aren't announced in advance; Hello Alice matches open
grants to your profile.
- [ ] Create an account and complete **every** profile field. Matching depends on it:
  - Owner identity: woman, minority (select every one that applies)
  - Industry: Retail / Apparel and Media / Creative
  - Location: [CITY], Orange County, California
  - Stage and revenue
- [ ] Turn on email notifications for grants
- [ ] Paste the "Tell us about your business" answer from `amber-grant.md`

## IFundWomen (ifundwomen.com)
Free. Fill out the universal grant application once and you're considered
for partner grants as they open. They require a for-profit US business with
fewer than 50 employees and under $5M in revenue.
- [ ] Create an account
- [ ] Complete the universal grant application using the answers in this folder
- [ ] Turn on notifications

## Also worth joining (free)
- [ ] OCIE SBDC (Cal State Fullerton): book a free advisor; ask about Orange County grants and help with the CPUC certification
- [ ] Your city's chamber of commerce newsletter (local grants show up there first)
