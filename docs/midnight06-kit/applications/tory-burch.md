# Tory Burch Foundation Fellows (2027 class)

- **Official site:** toryburchfoundation.org/fellows
- **Deadline:** **Nov 17, 2026, 11:59 PM Eastern**
- **What you get:** a year-long fellowship (education, network, a trip to a workshop) plus an education grant. Sources say $2,000-$5,000, so check the official rules
- **Fee:** free

## Eligibility: check this first
- [ ] **Business makes at least $75,000 a year** ← if not yet, skip this round and aim for next year
- [ ] At least 51% women-owned and controlled
- [ ] You own the largest (or equally largest) share and play a major management role
- [ ] 21+ by the deadline, English proficient, US legal resident
- [ ] For-profit, formed and operating in the US
- [ ] Only one founder per business may apply

## If eligible
This is competitive and the application is longer. Once your business profile
is filled in, Claude will draft every answer for the live form. Expect
questions about your growth plan, leadership, challenges and how the
fellowship would help you scale. A business plan is optional.

## If not eligible yet
Make $75,000 in yearly revenue a 2027 goal and put "Tory Burch Fellows, fall
2027" on your calendar. The Amber Grant, HerRise, Freed and Breva don't have
this revenue minimum.
