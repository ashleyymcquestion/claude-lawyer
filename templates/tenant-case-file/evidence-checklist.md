# Evidence checklist

Check each item off when you have a copy saved in the `evidence/` folder.
Never edit or crop the originals. Make copies if you want to mark them up.

## The rental
- [ ] The lease, and anything else you signed (addendums, move-in forms)
- [ ] Who is on the lease: you, your roommate, both?
- [ ] Landlord's or manager's name, address, phone and email
- [ ] How the landlord says rent should be paid (portal, check, address)

## What you paid
- [ ] Every payment to your roommate for rent: Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, bank transfer
      (screenshots that show the date, amount and note, plus the downloaded statement)
- [ ] Cash payments: any receipt, text or witness ("got your $1,200 for rent")
- [ ] Every payment you made straight to the landlord, with receipts

## What the roommate said
- [ ] Texts or messages where the roommate agreed to pay the landlord
- [ ] Texts where the roommate admits not paying, makes excuses or promises to pay
- [ ] Do not threaten the roommate. Keep messages short, calm and in writing.

## What the landlord sent
- [ ] Any notice (3-day notice, 30/60/90-day notice), with the date you got it,
      how it was delivered (handed to you, posted on the door, mailed) and a photo of it
- [ ] Rent ledger or balance the landlord says you owe
- [ ] Any court papers (summons, complaint). **If you got these, the response
      deadline is short. Run `paralegal deadline` and call a self-help center today.**

## Other
- [ ] Repair requests or problems with the unit (photos, dates)
- [ ] Police report number, if you filed one
